# CallIt v2 prototype

A clickable design prototype of CallIt, private group predictions: a phone app on phones and a desktop app on computers. Not the product; that is https://app.call-it.app.

- Opened from here, the page reads ESPN scoreboards, the TVmaze schedule, Wikipedia trends, Open-Meteo weather, and TikTok or YouTube link previews live. `feed.json` is the daily snapshot it starts from (`node sync-feed.mjs 7`).
- People, groups and picks are sample data.
- Film and photos: Pexels and Mixkit (free licenses). TV: TVmaze (CC BY-SA). Charts: Deezer. Weather: Open-Meteo. Trends: Wikimedia.
