CallIt · design round 8 · for Aviv, Yuval and Elad

The next call comes to you

The app opens on what's happening now in the things you picked: games, TV nights, award shows, family moments. Any of them becomes a call in two taps. Built for a dorm floor, a girls' group chat and a family thread alike.

Direction: Night Game

Chosen over Scoreboard and Group chat. Calls happen at night, during games, in a group chat, and this look is built for that moment. Coral is the brand sun and owns every action. Cyan marks your own pick. Pink means live.

New in round 8

  • One type system. Big Shoulders for headlines and numbers, Figtree for reading, one size scale. The serif is gone.
  • Film behind every section from licensed clips: games, watch nights, holiday tables, nights out, confetti.
  • Play. A live scorer with real padel and tennis scoring, plus ping pong and pickup, that settles the match's calls. Also duels and leagues between groups.
  • Real connections. Paste a TikTok or YouTube post to call its views. Wikipedia trends, the music chart, weather, Resy and OpenTable links, and Google Calendar all work. Strava needs setup. Instagram and Apple Health are not available on the web. See You → Connected apps.

New in round 6

  • A timeline until it happens. Shared links show picks lock in N, the start time and the result time. It's also on the answer page and after you post. In Telegram and Discord, a CallIt bot posts "1 hour to go", "it's live" and the result in the group.
  • Events update every day. sync-feed.mjs pulls the next 7 days of games and TV into feed.json, and the page loads it first. Live scores refresh every 20 seconds in a normal browser.
  • Every invite is its own page. The join page, sign-up, chat preview and answer page come from the group and the call behind the invite. Survivor Wed gets TV stage light, Hall 4B game footage, Family warm light in serif type, and Lions Den the NFL slate. Each shows its own inviter, open call, recent "called it" and calendar. Try the Invite buttons under Jump to.
  • Build plan. PLAN.md lists the work in order, for Yuval, Elad and Aviv, with every problem we expect and how to handle it.

New in round 4

  • Matches the device. On a phone it's the app, full screen. On a computer it's a desktop app with a sidebar, a feed, and a side rail for your turn and groups.
  • Every section has its own world. Sports runs on real footage of friends playing and cheering, with stadium light and camera flashes. TV night has stage lights and scanlines, award season gold dust, life and family warm light, and the world a turning globe.
  • Post your own call first. Making a call means picking your answer too. It publishes right away to your group only when you manage it, or goes to the manager as a suggestion. Then a confirmation shows your locked pick and when it unlocks.
  • College hoops and women's sports. Ranked NCAA men's and women's games from opening week (#20 Maryland at #1 South Carolina, #14 Miami vs #1 Florida), with an "Upset?" call. WNBA and NWSL are in the topic picker.
  • A new join page. "Maya invited you to Survivor Wed": film hero, the group's live calls, how it works, the brand line over a 1911 crowd, what's on the calendar, then a one-sheet sign-up.
  • Get 5 friends in, unlock the Season Pass. Group themes, ballot packs, season recap. No money involved.
  • Share anywhere. WhatsApp, Messages, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat or a link, each with a preview of what friends get.
  • Answer from the chat. Each answer is its own link. One tap in WhatsApp opens a one-screen page with that answer picked. No download, no account. A name keeps the pick.
  • On the lock screen. A live game you called sits there as a Live Activity.

New in round 3

  • Welcome in three steps. Pick your country (US first), then what you love from 39 topics across sports, reality TV, award shows, life and family, friends and the world. Then start or join a group.
  • A feed shaped by you. Sections reorder by your picks, with For you, Sports, TV, Life and World tabs on top. Change topics anytime in You.
  • TV calls from the real US schedule. Dancing with the Stars, Survivor, The Traitors, The Voice, Real Housewives, SNL and more, from TVmaze. Each show has its own kinds of call.
  • Season list. No free cast list exists, so each group keeps its own "who's still in" list, and it carries from episode to episode.
  • Life and family pools. Baby pool, Thanksgiving, wedding, first snow, and award season (CMA Awards Nov 18, Grammy nominations Nov 16).
  • New group in 3 steps. Pick a kind (Family, Watch party, Pick'em, Roommates, Office, Trip), then a name, emoji and color. Family groups get a gentle table and watch parties get auto calls, then you invite by link or QR.
  • A bigger "You called it." Rarity badge, the receipt (when you locked in), rank change, streak, friends' reactions and a shareable story card. A kind "Not this time" screen when you miss.
  • Alerts that name the call. A lock-screen preview: what's closing, who hasn't answered, results.
  • Still here from round 2: two-tap calls, house rules, live ESPN scores, quarter calls, Sunday slate, game night.
